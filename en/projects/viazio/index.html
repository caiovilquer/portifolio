RING

Decision problem

External APIs have different latencies, coverage, and failures. The ranking must remain useful when one criterion is missing and explain why each destination landed where it did.

SECTOR

Technical decisions

  1. Decomposable score

    Window quality, destination quality, and data confidence remain separate; weights are normalized when a source is unavailable.

  2. Provider isolation

    Independent retries, circuit breakers, and bulkheads prevent Wikipedia failures from taking down weather, currency, or holidays.

  3. PostgreSQL on the read path

    Reads use PostgreSQL, a two-second synchronous budget, and background refreshes instead of waiting on a slow upstream.
